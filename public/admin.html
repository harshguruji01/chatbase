Automated 30-Day Auto Purge Status

ChatBase runs an automated Postgres background cron schedule (cleanup-expired-messages-daily) every day at 3:00 AM UTC. All messages older than 30 days are automatically permanently deleted to keep database size optimal and maintain user privacy.

CRON JOB NAME
✓ cleanup-expired-messages-daily
SCHEDULE
Every day (0 3 * * *)
EXPIRATION POLICY
expires_at < now() (30 days hard ceiling)
